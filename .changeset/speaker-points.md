Speaker points: speakers earn 10 points per published Aaronism they are in, 1 per line they say, and 25 per published Aaronism they submitted under their own name. The Speakers page ranks them by points (with an A to Z toggle), and each speaker page shows their points, rank and who they need to pass next. Admins can correct an entry's submitter on its edit page.
